What the score means

A shortlist, not a verdict.

The fit score measures how closely each perfume matches the answers you gave. It is not a prediction that you will love the smell. Skin chemistry, memory and the weather still matter.

Deal-breakers receive the strongest penalty. Budget, scent direction and projection come next. Occasion, texture and practical priorities break close ties.

Sample first.

Wear a fragrance for several hours before buying a full bottle. The opening is only the first chapter.
