Three principles shaping this edit.

Short observations from the by.foro visual desk.

01

Build a reference library

Save what provokes a precise reaction, then return to it after the novelty has faded.

02

Follow the side door

Interesting discoveries often sit one name, credit or influence away from the thing you already know.

03

Delay the verdict

Taste becomes sharper when you can describe an object before deciding whether you like it.
