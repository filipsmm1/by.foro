Cookie policy

Effective 20 July 2026

You can accept or reject optional cookies at any time. Rejecting them does not prevent access to the site.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text records stored by a website in your browser. Similar browser storage can serve related purposes. Some storage is necessary for a requested service; optional storage requires a valid choice where applicable.

How consent works

Until you make a choice, Google Analytics and other optional scripts are blocked. “Accept optional” permits Analytics to load and place the optional cookies listed above. “Reject optional” keeps Analytics blocked and saves only the necessary preference cookie. The choice expires after 180 days, after which the site asks again.

Changing your choice

Select “Manage cookie preferences” on this page to reopen the controls. Saving a new choice replaces the old one immediately. You can also delete site data for byforo.com in your browser settings; the banner will then appear on your next visit.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics provides aggregated reports about page views, traffic sources, approximate geography, devices and engagement. Google may process information under its own terms and privacy documentation. Advertising personalisation is not enabled by this site implementation. If another optional service is added, this policy will be updated before activation.
